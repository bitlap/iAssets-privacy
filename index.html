iAssets Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

iAssets is a personal asset management app for stocks, deposits, wealth products and supported cryptocurrency account viewing. This policy explains data handling for the free app and Pro in-app purchases.

Local storage and iCloud

Your manually recorded holdings, operations, dividends, asset accounts, profit history and settings are stored on your device. Supported data also syncs to your own iCloud account when the system service is available. We do not operate developer servers to hold these asset ledgers. Apple provides iCloud storage and transmission; you can manage access and cloud files in system settings.

Pro purchases, subscriptions and payments

iAssets Pro offers monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices, currencies, billing periods and available plans are shown in the purchase screen and App Store confirmation. Apple processes payments through App Store In-App Purchase. The app does not read your Apple Account password, card number or full payment details.

The app uses Apple's StoreKit to verify Apple-signed purchase results on your device. It reads the product identifiers, entitlement expiry, refund or revocation status and billing grace-period status needed to unlock Pro, restore purchases and handle renewal or expiry. We do not separately upload these on-device entitlement verification details to developer servers or use them for advertising, tracking or profiling. The developer may receive sales and financial reports provided by Apple through App Store Connect.

Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can review, manage or cancel them in App Store subscription settings. Lifetime access does not renew, but purchasing it does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Canceling renewal normally preserves access for the paid period; expiry, refunds or revocation may affect subsequent Pro access. Apple handles refund requests under its applicable rules through Report a Problem.

Lifetime Pro for existing users

To identify users who acquired the app before its first monetized release, the app verifies Apple's AppTransaction on the device and compares the original acquisition date against a fixed release cutoff. Eligible existing users receive lifetime Pro without another payment. Eligibility is not based on local asset contents or imported files. This process does not disclose your Apple Account password or create a separate app account. If verification is temporarily unavailable, connect to the internet and use Restore Purchases; new purchases are temporarily disabled to avoid charging an eligible existing user.

Market data, exchange rates and third-party requests

The app requests stock search results and quotes directly from sources such as Eastmoney, Tencent and Yahoo Finance, exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API, and stock logos from sources such as Financial Modeling Prep and Stocktwits. Cryptocurrency valuation may also use exchange prices and Coinbase public exchange rates. Requests may contain search terms, stock symbols or currency codes; these public market-data requests do not include your manually recorded quantities, costs or complete asset ledger.

Providers receive network information necessary to serve requests, such as IP addresses, request times and parameters, and may log it under their own policies. Our not operating data servers does not mean third-party services process no data. Apple's payment, purchase-history and iCloud services are subject to the Apple Privacy Policy.

Cryptocurrency accounts and API credentials

If you choose to add a supported exchange connection (currently Binance), its API Key, API Secret and connection details are stored in the local iOS Keychain, not in the app's iCloud sync files or JSON exports. Your device sends queries directly to the exchange. Requests include the API Key, request parameters and required signatures; the API Secret generates signatures locally and is not sent in plain text. The exchange handles authentication and query information under its own rules.

The app only retrieves and displays balances, positions and valuations. It does not place orders, withdraw or transfer funds. Query results are displayed in session memory, not written to local asset snapshot files. Connection details and credentials remain until you remove the connection in the app. Prefer read-only keys and disable withdrawal and transfer permissions. You can also revoke a key at the exchange at any time.

Photos, files, import and export

You may choose a photo or file through a system picker for a custom stock. Selected images are displayed and cached locally; the app does not upload your photo library or obtain unrelated photo contents through this selection. You can manage relevant permissions in system settings. Declining photo access does not affect unrelated features.

Pro import and export read or write selected files only when you initiate the action. Exports may contain your asset ledger and settings, but not exchange API credentials or entitlement information that grants Pro. A pre-import rollback backup is kept locally. Keep exported files secure. If you choose a third-party cloud drive or share a file, subsequent handling depends on the destination and recipient you select.

Retention, deletion and your choices

You can delete stock or asset records and remove exchange connections in the app, and manage iCloud data and exported backups in system settings or Files. Uninstalling normally removes the local app container, but may leave iCloud files, exported copies or system Keychain items. Remove exchange connections before uninstalling and revoke exchange keys as needed. The app has no separate registered user account.

Subscription expiry does not automatically delete or truncate existing asset records, but it limits Pro-only features and additions above the free allowance. Apple retains its purchase history and transaction records under its policies and applicable obligations. Uninstalling the app or deleting local assets does not delete Apple's records or cancel an auto-renewable subscription. We do not hold developer-server asset ledgers to delete on your behalf. Contact us below if you need assistance.
