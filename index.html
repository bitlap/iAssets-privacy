iAssets 隐私政策

更新日期：2025年7月12日

iAssets（股票持仓）是一款个人股票持仓管理工具。我们重视您的隐私，本隐私政策说明了我们如何处理您的数据。

数据存储

您的所有投资数据（包括股票持仓、操作记录、派息记录等）均存储在您的设备本地以及您自有的 iCloud 账户中。我们不会在服务器端存储或传输您的任何个人投资数据。

第三方服务

App 使用以下第三方公开 API 获取行情与汇率数据，这些请求仅用于查询股票价格和汇率信息，不涉及您的个人数据：

• 东方财富 API — 用于股票搜索与代码查询
• 腾讯股票行情 API — 用于获取实时股价与涨跌幅
• ExchangeRate-API — 用于获取实时汇率数据

数据收集

本 App 不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人身份信息、设备信息或使用行为数据。App 不包含广告 SDK、分析 SDK 或任何第三方数据收集组件。

Apple 数据

App 可能使用 Apple 提供的标准功能（如 iCloud 同步）来备份和恢复您的数据，相关数据处理遵循 Apple 的隐私政策。

未成年人

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。如有重大变更，我们将在 App 内或 App Store 页面进行通知。

iAssets Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 12, 2025

iAssets (Stock Portfolio) is a personal stock portfolio management tool. We value your privacy, and this Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Storage

All your investment data (including stock holdings, transaction records, dividend records, etc.) is stored locally on your device and within your own iCloud account. We do not store or transmit any of your personal investment data on our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party public APIs to fetch market and exchange rate data. These requests are solely for retrieving stock prices and exchange rate information and do not involve your personal data:

• Eastmoney API — Used for stock search and symbol lookup
• Tencent Stock API — Used for real-time stock prices and price changes
• ExchangeRate-API — Used for real-time exchange rate data

Data Collection

This App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, device information, or usage behavior data. The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or any third-party data collection components.

Apple Data

The App may use standard Apple-provided features (such as iCloud sync) to back up and restore your data. Related data processing follows Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. In case of significant changes, we will notify users through the App or via the App Store listing.
